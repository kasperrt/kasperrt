Embroidery is an AI security platform designed for a world increasingly powered by autonomous agents. A lightweight endpoint application captures activity directly from AI tools running on employee devices, enabling Embroidery to detect malicious intent, unsafe behavior, and costly mistakes. Security teams can enrich this activity with audit logs from services such as Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, providing broader context for investigations across agents, users, tools, and systems.
